Privacy Policy — Starfire Homeward

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Developer: liuzhilin · Contact: liuzhilin1230@gmail.com

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Starfire Homeward (the "Game") on Android, distributed through Google Play.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The Game has no account system and no game server of its own. Your progress, settings and purchase records are stored only on your device. We do not receive, sell or share this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads, including optional rewarded videos, through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID (or app set ID);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, clicked, completed) and diagnostic data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and in other regions where required, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change this choice at any time from Settings → Privacy settings in the Game. Where you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalized ads.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details. The Game receives only the product, order ID and purchase token from Google Play and stores them on your device to deliver your items and to prevent duplicate delivery. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Retention and deletion

Game data on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game. Note that uninstalling also removes your progress, because it is not backed up to any server of ours. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies; you can manage it through your Google account and device settings.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through the controls above or directly with Google. You may still contact us with any question or request.

7. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the Game adds new features or services. The new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

8. Contact

liuzhilin — liuzhilin1230@gmail.com

隐私政策 — 星火归途

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日 · 开发者：liuzhilin · 联系方式：liuzhilin1230@gmail.com

本政策说明你在 Android 上（通过 Google Play 下载）游玩《星火归途》（以下简称"本游戏"）时涉及的信息处理。

1. 我们自己收集的信息

无。本游戏没有账号系统，也没有自己的游戏服务器。你的进度、设置和购买记录只保存在你的设备上。我们不会接收、出售或共享这些数据。

2. 广告（Google AdMob）

本游戏通过 Google AdMob 展示广告（包括可自愿观看的激励视频）。为投放和衡量广告、防止作弊，Google 可能收集和处理：

  • 设备广告 ID（或应用组 ID）；
  • IP 地址及由此推断的大致位置；
  • 设备和应用信息（机型、系统版本、语言、应用版本）；
  • 广告互动（展示、点击、看完）和诊断数据。

在欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士以及法律要求的其他地区，本游戏会通过 Google 用户消息平台（UMP）在展示个性化广告前征求你的同意。你可以随时在游戏的"设置 → 隐私设置"中更改选择。不同意时，Google 仍可能展示非个性化广告。

你可以在设备设置中重置广告 ID 或关闭个性化广告（设置 → Google → 广告，或 设置 → 隐私 → 广告）。了解 Google 如何使用数据：Google 如何使用合作伙伴网站或应用中的信息、Google 隐私权政策。

3. 应用内购买（Google Play 结算）

购买完全由 Google Play 处理，我们看不到你的银行卡或账单信息。本游戏只从 Google Play 收到商品、订单号和购买凭证，并保存在你的设备上，用于发放道具和防止重复发放。详见 Google 隐私权政策 和 Google 付款隐私权声明。

4. 儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集其个人信息。如果你认为有儿童通过本游戏提供了信息，请联系我们，我们会协助删除。

5. 保存与删除

清除应用数据或卸载本游戏即可删除设备上的游戏数据。注意：卸载也会删除你的进度，因为进度不会备份到我们的任何服务器。Google 收集的数据按 Google 的政策保存，你可以通过 Google 账号和设备设置管理。

6. 你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律（例如欧盟《通用数据保护条例》或美国加州《消费者隐私法》），你可能有权访问、更正、删除你的个人数据或反对对其的处理。由于我们自己不持有你的个人数据，大部分请求可以通过上述设置或直接向 Google 提出。你仍然可以就任何问题联系我们。

7. 政策更新

我们可能更新本政策（例如游戏增加新功能或服务时）。新版本会发布在本网址，并注明新的生效日期。
