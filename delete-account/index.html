Delete your Starfire Homeward account

Starfire Homeward by bigfish (developer: liuzhilin) · com.bigfish.starfirehomeward

Option 1 — in the Game (immediate)

  1. Open Starfire Homeward and sign in with the Google account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & cloud save → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and cloud save are deleted right away and you are signed out.

Option 2 — by email (if you no longer have the Game)

Send an email to liuzhilin1230@gmail.com with the subject Starfire Homeward account deletion, from the Google account (email address) you used to sign in to the Game, or state that address in the email.

We confirm the request by replying to that address and delete the account within 30 days. We will not ask for your password.

What is deleted

  • your account (email address, name, profile picture URL and Google account identifier received at sign-in);
  • your player ID, cloud save, earlier save copies and sync records.

What may be kept

  • records of in-app purchases (order ID, product, purchase token), only as long as needed to handle refunds, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations, at most 180 days after deletion unless the law requires longer;
  • data held by Google (Google Play purchases, advertising data) is managed under Google's own policies and your Google account settings.

Deleting your account does not remove the copy of the Game data on your device; clear the app's data or uninstall the Game to remove it. Purchased items cannot be restored after the account is deleted.

删除《星火归途》账号

《星火归途》（Starfire Homeward），bigfish（开发者：liuzhilin）· com.bigfish.starfirehomeward

方式一：在游戏内删除（立即生效）

  1. 打开《星火归途》，用要删除的 Google 账号登录。
  2. 进入 设置 → 账号与云存档 → 删除账号。
  3. 确认后，账号和云存档立即删除，并退出登录。

方式二：通过邮件申请（如果你已无法使用游戏）

请用登录游戏时使用的 Google 账号邮箱，向 liuzhilin1230@gmail.com 发送邮件，标题为 Starfire Homeward account deletion；如使用其他邮箱发送，请在邮件中写明登录用的邮箱地址。

我们会回复该邮箱确认请求，并在 30 天内完成删除。我们不会索要你的密码。

会被删除的数据

  • 你的账号（登录时获得的邮箱地址、姓名、头像链接和 Google 账号标识）；
  • 玩家 ID、云存档、旧存档副本和同步记录。

可能保留的数据

  • 应用内购买记录（订单号、商品、购买凭证），仅在处理退款、防欺诈和履行法律义务所需期间保留，除法律另有要求外，最长为删除后 180 天；
  • Google 持有的数据（Google Play 购买记录、广告数据）按 Google 自己的政策和你的 Google 账号设置管理。

删除账号不会删除设备上的游戏数据副本；如需删除，请清除应用数据或卸载游戏。账号删除后，已购买的物品无法恢复。
